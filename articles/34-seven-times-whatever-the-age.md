# 34 · Seven Times, Whatever the Age

> 题材：1693年哈雷的布雷斯劳生命表、为何用一座西里西亚小城的数，以及一张没人用的表
> 状态：**第三十四篇定稿**（2026-08-20）
> 指标：271 词 / 50 重点词 / 密度 18.5%
> 英国王室卖终身年金，价钱一律是年付额的七倍，不问买家几岁。哈雷1693年把定价方法摆了出来——七倍对每一个不满六十五岁的人都太少。那张表被摆出来、没人读，然后搁置；议会照旧收七倍。

## 英文正文

The English Crown **raised** money by **selling** life annuities. The price was seven times the **yearly** **payment**, whatever the **buyer's** age. A **lad** of ten and an **elderly** man **handed** over the same **sum**; the Crown counted the **inequity** as no concern of its own.

Subscribers saw the **loophole**. They **nominated** the **youngest** lives — **infants**, often their own **offspring** — and the **Exchequer** **sold** at a loss it could not measure. That age **mattered** was **uncontested**. Nobody could put a **quantity** on how much it mattered, because nobody had a table of how long people lived.

John Graunt had **combed** the London bills of mortality in 1662, but London did not note the age of the dead, and would not for a hundred years. Halley added a second **grounds**, in his own **wording**: London and Dublin were **swollen** by **strangers** who died there, **funerals** exceeding **births**, which **rendered** them **incapable** of being **standards**.

Breslau, in Silesia, could **serve**. A **cleric**, Caspar Neumann, had kept its parish **annals**, and they gave the age at death. The town was small and **stable**, some thirty-four thousand **souls**, with little **migration**. Neumann sent the **figures** by **courier** to the Royal Society. From five years of them Halley **assembled** a **roster**: of those born, a thousand reached one, 855 reached two, 107 lived past 85.

On 8 March 1693 he **submitted** the table to the Society; a week later, the **annuity** **tariff** **drawn** from it. Seven times was too little for every **purchaser** under **pension** age. The table was displayed, went **unread**, and was **shelved**. Parliament went on charging seven, **losing** on every life it **insured** young.

## 中文译文

> 铁律：英文加粗词在中文里对应的译法同样加粗，后附 `(english)` 括注词源。
> 中文按最自然的说法写，加粗落在自然说法上——不为对齐扭曲句法。

英国王室靠**卖**(selling)终身年金**筹**(raised)钱。价钱是**年**(yearly)**付额**(payment)的七倍，不问**买家的**(buyer's)岁数。一个十岁的**少年**(lad)和一位**上了年纪的**(elderly)人**交**(handed)出同一笔**钱**(sum)，而王室把这中间的**不公**(inequity)当作与己无关。

认购人看出了这个**空子**(loophole)。他们**指定**(nominated)**最年轻的**(youngest)人命——常常是自己的**婴孩**(infants)、自己的**子女**(offspring)——**国库**(Exchequer)就这样**卖**(sold)着，亏在哪里却算不出来。年龄**要紧**(mattered)，这**无人争辩**(uncontested)。可没人能给出一个**量**(quantity)，说清它到底要紧多少——因为没人手里有一张人能活多久的表。

1662年，约翰·格朗特**梳**(combed)过伦敦的死亡表；可伦敦不记死者的年龄，此后一百年也不记。哈雷又添了第二条**依据**(grounds)，用的是他自己的**措辞**(wording)：伦敦与都柏林被死在那里的**外来者**(strangers)**撑**(swollen)得虚胖，**葬礼**(funerals)多过**出生**(births)，这就**使**(rendered)它们**不配**(incapable)充当**标准**(standards)。

布雷斯劳可以，它在西里西亚，**当得起**(serve)。一位**教士**(cleric)卡斯帕尔·诺伊曼一直记着当地教区的**年录**(annals)，那上面写着死时的年龄。这座城小而**安定**(stable)，约莫三万四千**口人**(souls)，**流动**(migration)很少。诺伊曼托**信使**(courier)把**数字**(figures)送到皇家学会。就凭这五年的数，哈雷**拼**(assembled)出一张**名册**(roster)：一千人活到一岁，855人活到两岁，107人活过八十五。

1693年3月8日，他把这张表**提交**(submitted)给学会；一周后，从表里**推**(drawn)出的**年金**(annuity)**费率表**(tariff)也呈了上去。对每一个不到**养老**(pension)之年的**买主**(purchaser)来说，七倍都太少。这张表被摆出来，**没人读**(unread)，然后被**搁置**(shelved)。议会照旧收七倍，在每一条**保**(insured)得太年轻的人命上**亏**(losing)钱。

## 史实核对

以下关键信息已据所列来源核对：

- **已核（2026-08-20 动笔前逐条上网）**：英国政府售卖的终身年金**一律按年付额的七倍定价，不问年龄**（seven times the annual payment regardless of age）；而依哈雷的表，**直至 65 岁，正当价格都应高于七倍**——即政府对每一个未满 65 岁的购买者都定价过低。[Bellhouse, D.R. (2011) A new look at Halley's life table, *JRSS A* 174, 823-832](https://academic.oup.com/jrsssa/article/174/3/823/7077758)
- **已核 · §3 第一条理由**：约翰·格朗特 1662 年研究伦敦死亡表时，**伦敦并不记录死者年龄，直到 18 世纪才开始记录**。布雷斯劳则详细记有出生、死亡与死时年龄。[Actuarial News：Edmond Halley's Life Table and Its Uses](https://www.actuarial.news/2021/06/07/edmond-halleys-life-table-and-its-uses/)｜[Ciecka, J. (2008) Edmond Halley's Life Table and Its Uses](https://fac.comtech.depaul.edu/jciecka/Halley.pdf)
- **已核 · §3 第二条理由，用的是哈雷原话**：「both London and Dublin by reason of the great and casual Accession of Strangers who die therein, (as appeared in both, by the great Excess of the Funerals above the Births) rendred them incapable of being Standards for this purpose」——正文 swollen by strangers／funerals exceeding births／rendered them incapable of being standards 三处即据此。[Halley 1693 原文（pierre-marteau 编校本）](http://www.pierre-marteau.com/editions/1693-mortality.html)
- **已核 · 数据来路**：数据由布雷斯劳（今波兰弗罗茨瓦夫，时属哈布斯堡治下西里西亚）新教牧师卡斯帕尔·诺伊曼采自教区记录，年份为 **1687—1691**；传递路径为 **诺伊曼 → 莱布尼茨 → 朱斯泰尔 → 皇家学会 → 哈雷**。该城人口约 34,000，规模小、迁徙少，故可据以造表。[Bellhouse 2011](https://academic.oup.com/jrsssa/article/174/3/823/7077758)｜[Halley's life table (1693), Springer](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-0-85729-115-8_2)
- **已核 · 表中三个数字**：**1000** 人活到一岁（约当 1100 余名活产），**855** 人活到两岁，798 人活到三岁；哈雷另注明 **85 至 100 岁之间尚存 107 人**。正文取 1000／855／107 三数。[Bellhouse 2011](https://academic.oup.com/jrsssa/article/174/3/823/7077758)｜[HistData：Halley's Breslau Life Table](https://friendly.github.io/HistData/reference/Breslau.html)
- **已核 · 日期**：**1693年3月8日**哈雷在皇家学会会议上出示死亡率表，**一周后**出示据此算得的年金价格。论文载于《自然科学会报》第17卷 596—610 页，题为 An estimate of the degrees of the mortality of mankind, drawn from curious tables of the births and funerals at the city of Breslaw; with an attempt to ascertain the price of annuities upon lives。[Bellhouse 2011（PDF 全文）](https://jhanley.biostat.mcgill.ca/c609/material/BellhouseHalleyTable2011JRSS.pdf)
- **本篇的机制边界**：哈雷需要的不是更多数据，而是**一种特定的数据**——记有死时年龄、且人口进出稳定的城镇。伦敦两样都不具备：不记年龄，且被外来人口涨破。**判别性证据因此不在数据量上，而在数据的种类上。**
