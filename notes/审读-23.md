## 观众一 · 专业英文评鉴师
1. §5 «the man talked, and his bands fitted both **crimes**» his 最近先行词是 the man（工友）。改：Pitchfork's bands。【A】
2. §2 «a Home Office that **denied** the boy **refused** **entry** was hers» 连续省略，要读三遍。改：that denied that the boy refused entry was hers。【A】
3. §1 «some **rungs** shared between **kin** and child» kin 包含 child。改：shared by kin, parent with child。【B】
4. stronger／strongest 两个名额同词元。【B】
5. 中文数字无空格。【C】
