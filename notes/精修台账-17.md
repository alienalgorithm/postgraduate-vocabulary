## 轮次记录

### 第9轮 · 已完成 · 整体冷读

④刁钻一问：「一在8000」——**精确核实**：Geiger & Marsden 1909 原文写「Three different determinations showed that of the incident α-particles about 1 in 8000 was reflected」；且铂箔正是每8000个中一个偏转超90°（arXiv 1202.0954）。正文写 platinum test，与原文一致 ✓。暗室三十分钟亦已核：观察者先在全黑房间坐半小时适应视觉再用显微镜逐个计数 ✓。
⑤最弱一句：§5「Their empirical framework linked observation to prediction.」与「A textbook narrative compresses the sequence into a single revelation.」——两处均为抽象名词作主语，**而这一段讲的正是「三个人分头做成的」，主语理应是那三个人**。已分别改为 Between them they built an empirical framework linking... 与 Textbooks retell it as a single revelation.
另削一处赘语：returning to the side of incidence → returning towards incidence（入射侧就是入射方向）。
