§3「He could not explain it, **having borrowed** every formula from his northern colleague.」
——冷读时这句让我卡住：**「借用公式」与「解释不了3.24秒」之间没有因果**。
借公式不会导致测不准，它与这处误差无关，而 having 把两件事挂成了因果。
真实关系是：**借了公式，所以更怕露怯**——那是羞耻的来源，

已改为：He could not explain it. He had borrowed every formula from his northern
colleague, and under a regime that shot men on suspicion, an admission meant
disgrace; he hid the figure and lived in dread of the reckoning.
**「解释不了」独立成句后更重，而「借公式」归入了它真正所属的那条链。**
