# 审读 · 第38篇《He Made It Wear Their Words》

> **人读**（2026-09-04，沙箱无密钥，由模型分饰五角照章程读）。

## 观众一 · 专业英文评鉴师

1. §2 «Napoleon… called the **overhaul** **tormenting** the people with **trivia**» call X tormenting 不成句。改：said the overhaul was tormenting the people with trivia。【A】
2. §5 «He had made it **wear** their words **thereby**» thereby 句末悬着。改：He had thereby made it wear their words。【A】
3. §5 «**remarked** the **swap**» 英式「注意到」，可。【C】
4. §4 «the **patterns** in Paris» 术语（标准器），中文有。【C】
5. 中文数字无空格。【C】

## 观众二 · 英语母语读者

- «called the overhaul tormenting the people» 不通。
- «Yet read as a concession, it was not.» 好。«Only its top grade was pinned.» 好。
- «the patterns in Paris» patterns？哦，标准器。
- «wear their words thereby» thereby 放末尾僵。
