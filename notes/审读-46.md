## 观众一 · 专业英文评鉴师

1. §1 «three **sprinters** were hand-timed… and all three were **ratified** as world records» 被认定为纪录的是成绩不是人。改：all three times were ratified。【A】
2. §3 «A **timekeeper** **trips** on the **muzzle** smoke, which he must answer» trips 缺宾语（trips his watch）；which he must answer 悬着。改：trips his watch on the muzzle smoke, a cue he must answer, and stops it on…。【A】
3. §3 «the body fixed the **conversion**» the body 指管理机构，读者不知。改：the governing body。【A】
4. 中文数字无空格。【C】

## 观众四 · 硕士生

1. 不值得背：watches、tie、chests、finish、shared、pile、edge、raced、undo——九个基础词。【B】
2. 括注：**§3 中文「在枪口冒烟(muzzle)时」——括注挂在「冒烟」上，muzzle 是枪口。**改「在**枪口**(muzzle)冒烟时」。edge「便宜」改「优势」；unearned「白得了一点」不是词，改「不劳而获的」；scorer「记错」改「计分员」。【A】
