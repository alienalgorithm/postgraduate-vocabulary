考研英语精读 · No.22

The Building That Melted a Car

会烤化汽车的大楼

On 2 September 2013 the news broke that a London skyscraper under construction had damaged a Jaguar. Martin Lindsay had left the saloon by the pavement on Eastcheap, opposite the tower's frontage. He returned to find its wing mirror warped, its panels deformed and its badge distorted. The glazed facade above was to blame.

2013年9月2日，一则新闻传开，说伦敦一座在建的摩天大楼损坏了一辆捷豹车。马丁·林赛把这辆轿车停在伊斯特奇普街的人行道边，正对着这座高楼的正面。回来时他发现，车的后视镜已经翘曲，车身板变形，车标也扭曲了。罪魁祸首是上方那面镶玻璃的外墙。

Rafael Viñoly curved the south face of 20 Fenchurch Street inward, enlarging the upper storeys, where tenants pay a premium. That geometry acted like a magnifying glass, focusing sunlight into a beam up to six times brighter than direct sun. It was measured at about 110 degrees Celsius, and a reporter fried an egg in a pan using the reflection.

拉斐尔·维诺利把芬丘奇街20号的南立面设计成向内弯曲，扩大了高处的楼层，那里的租户要付溢价。这种几何形状就像一面放大镜，把阳光汇聚成一道光束，亮度可达直射阳光的六倍。测得的温度约为110摄氏度，一名记者还在平底锅里利用反射光煎熟了一个鸡蛋。

The developers issued an apology and reimbursed almost 1,000 pounds for repairs. Parking bays were suspended and a provisional barrier was erected. The beam struck for about two hours a day, two to three weeks annually, because of the sun's angle of elevation. Newspapers dubbed the Walkie-Talkie the Walkie-Scorchie, a taunt.

开发商公开道歉，并赔付了近1,000英镑的修理费。路边停车位暂停使用，还架起了一道临时性的屏障。受这一时节太阳的角度与高度影响，这道光束每年有两到三周、每天约两个小时照到街上。报纸把这座"对讲机"大楼叫作"烤焦机"，这是一句嘲讽。

The architect was candid, admitting many mistakes. Viñoly had anticipated the focusing, he said, but not its intensity. Sun louvres in the original design were reportedly removed in cost-cutting. Nor was this his first architectural miscalculation. In 2010 the infamous glare from his curved, reflective Vdara hotel in Las Vegas burned a guest's scalp by the pool.

这位建筑师很坦率，承认犯了很多错误。他说，他预料到外墙会聚光，却没想到聚光的强度会这么大。据报道，原设计中的遮阳百叶在削减成本时被删掉了。这也不是他第一次出现建筑上的失算。2010年，他设计的拉斯维加斯维达拉酒店外墙弯曲、反光，那臭名昭著的强光在泳池边灼伤了一位客人的头皮。

On 12 February 2014 a permanent fixture was announced: horizontal aluminium fins to diffuse the brightness, reportedly costing several million pounds. In September 2015 Building Design's critics gave the notorious tower the Carbuncle Cup, a trophy for the UK's worst new building. The ballot was unanimous.

2014年2月12日，一项永久性的固定装置方案公布，那就是在外墙加装水平的铝制散光鳍片来散射光线、减弱亮度，据报道花费达数百万英镑。2015年9月，《建筑设计》杂志的评论家们把这座声名狼藉的大楼评为"烂疮杯"得主，这一奖杯颁给英国最糟糕的新建筑。这次投票是全体一致的结果。
