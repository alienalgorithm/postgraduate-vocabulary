考研英语精读 · No.52

The Killer Smog of 1952

夺命毒雾：伦敦1952

On Friday 5 December 1952, cold, stagnant air descended on London. Overhead, warmer air pressed down like a lid, holding the chill captive beneath. Londoners were accustomed to murky, dreary winters. This time, though, the capital's smoke was trapped too, thickening into a choking smog, a mix of smoke and fog.

1952年12月5日，星期五，寒冷而凝滞的空气降临伦敦。头顶上方，较暖的空气像盖子一样压下来，把寒气困在下面。伦敦人早已习惯冬天昏暗、阴沉。不过这一次，首都的煤烟也被困住了，越积越浓，成了令人窒息的烟雾，也就是烟与雾的混合物。

That smoke, from coal burned in domestic fireplaces and power stations, was laden with soot and noxious sulphur dioxide. In places, visibility fell to a few metres. Ambulance services were suspended, an opera at the Sadler's Wells theatre was abandoned at the interval, and prize cattle at the Smithfield agricultural show died or had to be slaughtered.

这些煤烟来自家庭壁炉和发电厂烧的煤，满含烟尘和有毒的二氧化硫。有些地方，能见度降到只有几米。救护车服务暂停；萨德勒之井剧院（Sadler's Wells）的一场歌剧演到幕间休息被迫中止；史密斯菲尔德农业展会上的获奖的牛有的死去，有的只得宰杀。

The smog dissipated on Tuesday 9 December. Officials at the time put the excess deaths at about 4,000, but a 1954 Ministry of Health report blamed later deaths on influenza. A study published in Environmental Health Perspectives in 2001 reassessed the evidence and cast doubt on that explanation, estimating about 12,000 excess deaths between December 1952 and February 1953.

12月9日，星期二，烟雾消散。当时的官员估计超额死亡约4,000人，但卫生部1954年的一份报告把后来的死亡归因于流感。2001年发表于《环境健康展望》（Environmental Health Perspectives）的一项研究重新评估了证据，对这一解释提出质疑，估计1952年12月至1953年2月间超额死亡约12,000人。

For decades Londoners had tolerated the smoke as a nuisance. One villain flickered in their own grates, a source of warmth and comfort so habitual that few thought to accuse it. Such complacency may help to explain the government's grudging response. It withstood pressure for an inquiry until May 1953.

几十年来，伦敦人一直把煤烟看成一种麻烦，容忍了下来。有一个元凶就在他们自家的炉栅里摇曳，它带来温暖与舒适，人们早已习以为常，很少有人想到要指责它。这种麻痹大意或许有助于说明，政府为何勉强作出回应。政府一直顶住要求调查的压力，直到1953年5月才松口。

Reform proved protracted, slowed by official inertia. The Clean Air Act became law on 5 July 1956, three and a half years after the smog. It empowered municipal councils to create smokeless zones, combating smoke at its source. The cosy hearth, beloved of British families, had, all along, been among the chief offenders.

改革旷日持久，又因官方的惰性而进展迟缓。1956年7月5日，《清洁空气法》成为法律，距那场烟雾已有三年半。该法授权市镇议会设立无烟区，从源头上遏制煤烟。英国家庭深受喜爱的、温馨的炉膛，原来一直都是主要的肇事者之一。
