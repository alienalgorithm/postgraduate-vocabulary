考研英语精读 · No.16

The Bread Made from Air

从空气中造面包

In July 1909, BASF envoys gathered round a squat machine on a bench in Fritz Haber's Karlsruhe laboratory. Behind its gleaming, bolted valves, nitrogen and hydrogen met at crushing pressure and furnace heat, and transparent beads of ammonia began to drip slowly. Haber had pried nourishment from the air.

1909年7月，在弗里茨·哈伯位于卡尔斯鲁厄的实验室里，BASF的特使们围着工作台上一台矮墩墩的机器。在闪闪发亮、用螺栓紧固的阀门后面，氮和氢在巨大的压力和熔炉般的高温下相遇，合成氨凝成透明的液珠，开始缓缓地滴落。哈伯从空气中撬出了养分。

BASF's Carl Bosch scaled the method up, and from 1913 a gigantic plant at Oppau mass-produced ammonia. Hailed for making bread from air, Haber won the coveted 1918 Nobel Prize. Ploughed into stony soils as fertiliser, ammonia wards off famine as no manure heap could, bringing abundance. By 2008 it was feeding nearly half of mankind.

BASF的卡尔·博施把这一方法放大，从1913年起，奥保一座庞大的工厂开始大规模生产氨。哈伯因"从空气中造面包"而受到赞誉，获得了令人艳羡的1918年诺贝尔奖。氨作为化肥被翻耕进多石的土壤，抵御着饥荒，带来丰饶，这是任何粪肥垒成的肥堆都做不到的。到2008年，它养活了将近一半的人类。

Haber's ambition had a sinister side. Ammonia stocked Germany's arsenal with nitrates for munitions despite a British blockade, and Captain Haber marshalled chemical warfare. On 22 April 1915 at Ypres, troops vented about 168 tonnes of greenish, poisonous chlorine towards the Allied trenches, the first large-scale gas onslaught.

哈伯的雄心也有阴暗的一面。尽管英国实行封锁，合成氨仍为制造弹药所需的硝酸盐提供了来源，充实了德国的军火库；哈伯上尉则统筹指挥了化学战。1915年4月22日，在伊普尔，德军朝协约国的战壕方向释放了约168吨黄绿色、有毒的氯气，这是第一次大规模的毒气攻击。

Haber's wife, Clara Immerwahr, Germany's first woman with a doctoral degree in chemistry, voiced objections to his work. On 2 May 1915, after festivities in his honour, she shot herself in the garden with his service pistol. The next day the widower left for the Eastern Front.

哈伯的妻子克拉拉·伊梅瓦尔是德国第一位获得化学博士学位的女性，她对他的工作提出过反对。1915年5月2日，在为他举行的庆功活动之后，她在花园里用他的军用手枪自尽。第二天，这位丧妻之人动身前往东线。

Prestige offered no sanctuary. Of Jewish origin, Haber left Germany an outcast after Hitler's regime seized power, dying in Basel in 1934. His institute's pesticide research had produced Zyklon. A later variant, Zyklon B, was used in Nazi death camps, where relatives of his died.

声望没有给他带来庇护。哈伯是犹太裔，在希特勒政权随即夺取权力之后，他作为一个被放逐者离开德国，1934年死于巴塞尔。他研究所的杀虫剂研究曾催生齐克隆。后来的变体齐克隆B被用于纳粹死亡营，他的一些亲属就死在那里。

About half the nitrogen in our proteins and DNA has passed through a Haber-Bosch factory, so each of us carries a little of Haber's chemistry.

我们的蛋白质和DNA中约有一半的氮经过了哈伯-博施工厂，所以我们每个人身上都带着一点哈伯的化学。
