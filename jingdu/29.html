考研英语精读 · No.29

The Volcano That Gave Us Frankenstein

一座火山，造出了《弗兰肯斯坦》

In June 1816 a ceaseless downpour kept a party besieged at the Villa Diodati, a mansion the poet Lord Byron had leased on the outskirts of Geneva. After reading an anthology of German ghost stories in French translation, Byron proposed that each guest write a supernatural tale.

1816年6月，一场连绵不断的大雨，把一群人围困在迪奥达蒂别墅里。这座宅邸位于日内瓦郊外，是诗人拜伦勋爵租下的。读完一本德国鬼故事的选集的法文译本之后，拜伦提议每位客人各写一个超自然故事。

The melancholy weather was attributable to a distant blast. On 10 April 1815 Mount Tambora, on the Indonesian island of Sumbawa, blew itself apart in one of the most potent eruptions in recorded history. About 10,000 people died immediately, and tens of thousands more of starvation and disease in the aftermath.

这种阴郁的天气，源于远方的一次爆炸。1815年4月10日，印度尼西亚松巴哇岛上的坦博拉火山炸裂开来，那是有记录以来最猛烈的喷发之一。约1万人当场死亡，在随后的余波中，又有数万人死于饥饿和疾病。

The volcano pumped prodigious quantities of sulphur dioxide from its crater into the stratosphere, where it formed sulphate aerosols that blanketed the globe, reflecting sunshine and thereby lowering temperatures world-wide. Europe and North America came to know 1816 as the Year Without a Summer.

这座火山从火山口向平流层泵入了数量惊人的二氧化硫，它们在那里形成硫酸盐气溶胶，像毯子一样笼罩全球，反射阳光，从而使全世界的气温降低。欧洲和北美后来把1816年称作无夏之年。

Europe's summer was chilly and relentlessly wet. Crops failed or lay rotten in saturated fields, cereal and bread prices escalated, and scarcity, disease and unrest followed.

欧洲的夏天寒冷，雨也下个不停。庄稼要么歉收，要么腐烂在浸透了水的田地里，谷物和面包价格飞涨，接着出现了短缺、疾病和动荡。

The eighteen-year-old Mary Godwin, later Mary Shelley, had set out in May alongside Percy Bysshe Shelley and Claire Clairmont. In her 1831 preface she would recollect a damp, gloomy summer whose torrents often kept the group indoors for days.

十八岁的玛丽·戈德温，也就是后来的玛丽·雪莱，5月间与珀西·比希·雪莱和克莱尔·克莱蒙特结伴启程。在1831年的序言里，她回忆说，那是一个潮湿、阴沉的夏天，倾盆大雨常常把大家困在屋里好几天。

Byron's challenge proved fruitful. His physician John Polidori produced The Vampyre, whilst Byron's poetic output included Darkness. Mary began the ghastly yarn that became Frankenstein, which appeared anonymously in 1818 and was later reissued under her name. It is often called the founding work of the science-fiction genre.

拜伦的挑战成果丰硕。他的医生约翰·波利多里写出了《吸血鬼》，与此同时，拜伦诗歌方面的产出中有《黑暗》。玛丽开始写那个恐怖的故事，它后来成了《弗兰肯斯坦》，1818年匿名出版，后来又以她的名字再版。这部作品常被称为科幻体裁的开山之作。

A tropical volcano had supplied the rain, and a teenager the monster.

一座热带火山提供了雨，一个少女提供了怪物。
