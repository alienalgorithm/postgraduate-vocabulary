The Last Flight of the Hindenburg

兴登堡号的 34 秒

In 1936 the colossal Hindenburg made 17 Atlantic round trips, a marvel of luxury and glamour. Its amenities included a lounge and, that year, a grand piano. An American embargo on helium export had left the ship relying on hydrogen. Because that gas was combustible, smokers sat behind an airlock with one electric lighter.

1936年，巨大的兴登堡号完成了17次跨大西洋往返飞行，是奢华与魅力的奇观。它的设施包括一间休息厅，那一年还有一架三角钢琴。美国对氦气出口实行禁运，这艘飞艇只能依靠氢气。由于氢气易燃，吸烟者只能坐在一道气闸后面，那里只有一个电点火器。

The airship left Frankfurt on 3 May 1937. Hours behind its timetable, it met thunderstorms at Lakehurst and circled for over an hour before its descent towards the mooring mast. At about 7:25 pm on 6 May, flames appeared near the stern. The inferno incinerated the ship in about half a minute, leaving a crumpled frame.

1937年5月3日，飞艇离开法兰克福。它比时刻表晚了几个小时，在莱克赫斯特遇到雷暴，盘旋了一个多小时，才开始向系留塔架下降。5月6日晚约7时25分，飞艇尾部附近出现了火焰。这场烈火在约半分钟内把飞艇焚毁，只留下一副扭曲变形的骨架。

Of the 97 aboard, 62 survived, a proportion close to two-thirds, while a minority of 35 died, as did one man on the ground. A 14-year-old cabin boy was drenched by a deluge from a burst water tank, and the soaking saved him. He dropped through a hatch and scrambled clear, escaping only narrowly.

艇上97人中有62人生还，比例接近三分之二；遇难的35人是少数，地面上另有一名男子丧生。一名14岁的侍应生被一个破裂水箱倾泻而下的水浇透，正是这一身湿透救了他。他从一个舱口钻下去，连滚带爬地逃开，侥幸逃过一劫。

Newsreel cameras and photographers caught the disaster. A radio correspondent's eyewitness commentary was not live; audiences heard it the next day. The cause has not been definitively established, but static electricity igniting leaking hydrogen is the leading explanation. A theory blaming the doped fabric has been rejected.

新闻纪录片摄影机和摄影师拍下了这场灾难。一位电台通讯员的目击解说并不是直播，听众第二天才听到。起火原因至今没有确切查明，但最主要的解释是静电点燃了泄漏的氢气。一种归咎于上过涂料的蒙皮织物的说法已被否定。

The 1933 crash of USS Akron killed 73 yet won less notoriety. The filmed fire left a fateful image that abruptly wrecked confidence. As public perception soured, Graf Zeppelin was withdrawn, later scrapped, and a glamorous epoch of aviation closed. The doomed airship's first journey to North America had begun on 6 May 1936, exactly a year before the fire.

1933年"阿克伦号"失事造成73人死亡，恶名却没那么大。被拍下的大火留下了一幅影响深远的画面，骤然摧毁了人们的信心。公众观感变差后，"格拉夫·齐柏林号"退役，后来被拆毁，航空史上一个光彩夺目的时代就此结束。这艘终将毁于大火的飞艇首次飞往北美的航程始于1936年5月6日，恰好在那场大火的一年前。
