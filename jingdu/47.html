考研英语精读 · No.47

Shadows of Wolves Over Yellowstone

黄石公园狼影再现

Yellowstone's iconic grey wolves had been exterminated by 1926. On 12 January 1995 eight wolves captured in Alberta, Canada, were hauled to the park in crates; six more followed. Released from enclosures in late March, they roamed the wilderness.

黄石公园那些标志性的灰狼，到1926年已遭赶尽杀绝。1995年1月12日，八只在加拿大艾伯塔省捕获的狼装在板条箱里，一路拖运进了公园；随后又来了六只。3月下旬，它们被放出围栏，得以在荒野中四处游荡。

In February 2014 a viral video narrated by writer George Monbiot spun a compelling narrative: elk, thinned by wolves and wary of them, retreated from valleys, letting willows flourish and riverbanks stabilise. Ecologists call this a trophic cascade: predators eat or deter plant-eaters, and vegetation rebounds.

2014年2月，一段由作家乔治·蒙比奥特解说的视频在网上疯传，编织出一个引人入胜的故事。片中说，麋鹿被狼捕杀得数量变少，又处处提防着狼，便撤离了河谷，于是柳树繁茂起来，河岸也稳固下来。生态学家把这称为营养级联：捕食者吃掉或吓阻食草动物，植被便恢复生长。

Yet the census undermined the thinning claim: elk on the park's northern range peaked at about 19,000 in January 1994 and were dwindling before wolves arrived, depleted by hunters, drought and bears. Fear looked weak too: in June 2013 Arthur Middleton's University of Wyoming team found that collared elk east of the park kept grazing unless wolves ventured within half a mile.

然而，普查结果首先就削弱了“鹿被狼吃少了”这一说法。黄石北部草场的麋鹿在1994年1月达到约19,000头的峰值，狼还没来就已日渐减少；猎人、干旱和熊不断削减着鹿群。“恐惧”一说看来也站不住。2013年6月，阿瑟·米德尔顿领导的怀俄明大学团队发现，园区东侧戴项圈的麋鹿照样吃草，除非狼闯进半英里以内。

Trees fared no better: in November 2021 Utah State University's Elaine Brice showed that sampling only the tallest aspens overstated regrowth four- to sevenfold. The deeper flaw was water. Colorado State University's Tom Hobbs and David Cooper had excluded elk with fences and built artificial beaver dams. In January 2024 they reported: eroding streams had cut so deep that stunted willows no longer reached water.

树木方面的证据也好不到哪里去：2021年11月，犹他州立大学的伊莱恩·布赖斯指出，只抽样测量最高的山杨，会把它们的再生程度夸大四到七倍。更根本的漏洞出在水上。科罗拉多州立大学的汤姆·霍布斯和戴维·库珀用围栏把麋鹿挡在外面，并修建人工海狸坝。2024年1月，他们报告说，不断冲蚀河床的溪流下切太深，那些矮小的柳树已经够不着水了。

In January 2025 Oregon State University's William Ripple, an advocate of the hypothesis, reported one of the world's strongest cascades; critics disputed his methods. The question is unresolved, but the wolves' shadow looks fainter than the fable suggested, and the science messier. A discipline willing to revise its cherished example may deserve more trust than the fable ever did.

2025年1月，这一假说的倡导者、俄勒冈州立大学的威廉·里普尔发文称，黄石的级联效应位居世界最强之列；批评者则对他的方法提出质疑。问题仍悬而未决，但狼投下的影子比寓言所说的要淡，相关研究也要复杂纠结得多。一门肯修正自己珍视的案例的学科，或许比那则寓言更值得信赖。
