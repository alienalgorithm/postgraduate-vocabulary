考研英语精读 · No.07

The Octopus That Escaped

越狱的章鱼

One night in early 2016, after keepers at New Zealand's National Aquarium in Napier had left, an octopus named Inky crept through an aperture in the mesh atop its tank. The fugitive presumably dropped to the floor, crossed the room and compressed its elastic body into a drain, whose pipe stretched some 50 metres to the Pacific.

2016年初的一个夜晚，新西兰内皮尔国家水族馆的饲养员下班离开后，一只名叫 Inky 的章鱼从水箱顶部网罩上的一道开口悄悄爬出。这名逃犯想必随后落到地面，爬过房间，把富有弹性的身体压扁，挤进一个排水口；排水口下面那条管道延伸约50米，直通太平洋。

Staff reconstructed that route from the slime residue it deposited. Inky had resided in captivity since 2014, when a fisherman retrieved it from a crayfish pot off Napier. The breakout went unreported for about three months. Made public in April 2016, the story travelled the globe.

工作人员根据它留下的黏液残迹，还原了这条路线。Inky 自2014年起就生活在圈养环境中；那一年，一名渔民在内皮尔附近海域的捕龙虾笼子里捞到了它。这次出逃约三个月都无人报道。2016年4月消息公开后，这件事传遍了全球。

Such agility stems from an octopus's pliable body, which has no skeleton or shell. Its only hard part is its beak, so any crevice that accommodates the beak will do. Roughly two-thirds of its 500 million neurons lie in the limbs, which handle sensory input and react with some autonomy.

如此灵巧的身手源于章鱼柔韧的身体，它没有骨骼，也没有外壳。它唯一坚硬的部位是喙部，所以任何容得下喙部的裂缝都行。章鱼的5亿个神经元中，约三分之二分布在腕足里；腕足能处理感官信息，并在一定程度上自主作出反应。

Its wits are as versatile as its anatomy. A giant Pacific octopus in Seattle learned to open screw-top jars, needing 15 minutes at the outset, later about two. In a 2010 study, octopuses came to discriminate between two strangers, a feeder and one who touched them with a bristly stick, adopting a different hue and posture for each.

章鱼的才智和它的身体构造一样灵活多变。西雅图的一只巨型太平洋章鱼学会了拧开螺旋盖罐子，起初要花15分钟，后来约两分钟。在2010年的一项研究中，章鱼逐渐学会区分两个陌生人，一个是喂食者，另一个用带刺毛的棍子触碰它们；章鱼面对两人时呈现出不同的体色和姿态。

In July 2012 the Cambridge Declaration on Consciousness listed octopuses among animals possessing the neurological substrates for conscious awareness. A November 2021 UK government review recommended treating them as sentient, and the Animal Welfare (Sentience) Act encompassed them in April 2022. None of this proves an inner life. Inky, somewhere beyond the pipe, remained wholly oblivious to the debate.

2012年7月，《剑桥意识宣言》把章鱼列入具备有意识觉知所需神经基础的动物之列。2021年11月，一份为英国政府所做的评估报告建议把章鱼视为有感知力的动物；2022年4月，英国《动物福利（感知）法》将章鱼纳入其中。这些都不能证明章鱼有内心世界。Inky 身在管道另一头的某个地方，对这场争论全然不知。
