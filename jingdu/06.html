考研英语精读 · No.06

The Horse That Could Count

会算术的马

Wilhelm von Osten, a mathematics teacher and trainer in Berlin, owned a stallion, Hans. Asked to calculate a sum, attempt subtraction or name a date, Hans tapped out the answer with a hoof. Such aptitude suggested brilliance, and demonstrations in his owner's courtyard made him seem intelligent. The New York Times reported the stunning act in 1904.

冯·奥斯滕是柏林的一名数学教师和驯马师，他有一匹名叫汉斯的公马。让汉斯计算一道算式、试做减法或说出一个日期，它就会用蹄子敲出答案。这样的天资显示出一种聪慧，在主人庭院里的表演也让它看上去很聪明。1904年，《纽约时报》报道了这一令人惊叹的表演。

A commission of thirteen was formed to investigate. It included a veterinarian, a circus manager, a cavalry officer, schoolteachers and the director of the Berlin zoological garden. Its members looked for trickery and collusion. In September 1904 they found no deception, in effect certifying the act authentic.

一个13人委员会成立，负责调查。成员包括一名兽医、一名马戏团经理、一名骑兵军官、几名中小学教师，以及柏林动物园园长。委员们查找诡计和串通。1904年9月，他们没有发现任何骗局，实际上等于认证了这场表演货真价实。

In 1907 the psychologist Oskar Pfungst tested the act systematically, altering one variable after another. He kept onlookers away, rotated questioners, fitted blinders and sometimes used people ignorant of the answer. When Hans could see the questioner, he answered correctly 50 times in 56. With the questioner invisible, his accuracy fell to two in 35.

1907年，心理学家奥斯卡·普丰斯特对这一表演进行了系统测试，逐一改变各个变量。他让围观者远离，轮换提问者，给马戴上眼罩，有时还找对答案一无所知的人来提问。汉斯能看见提问者时，56次中答对了50次；提问者不在视线内时，它的准确率降到35次中仅对2次。

The cues came from the questioners. Each would lean forward as tapping began, and tension rose as the taps neared the answer. At the right tap it eased, and the questioner would straighten or jerk the head upward. Every gesture and motion was subconscious, made innocently by people with no wish to deceive.

提示来自提问者。敲击开始时，每个提问者都会前倾身体；敲击越接近答案，紧张就越强。敲到正确的那一下时，紧张随即缓解，提问者会挺直身子，或把头向上猛地一抬。每个手势和动作都出于下意识，都是无心做出的，做出这些动作的人并不想欺骗。

Double-blind designs in psychology and animal cognition guard against the Clever Hans effect, a phenomenon of bias in which expectancy shapes behavioural results. In the laboratory, Pfungst took the animal's place and proved sensitive to the cues of attentive volunteers. Hans went, on his owner's death in June 1909, to a jeweller still convinced that such animals reasoned.

心理学和动物认知研究中的双盲设计，正是为了防范"聪明汉斯效应"。这是一种偏差现象，指预期会影响行为结果。在实验室里，普丰斯特亲自扮演马的角色，事实证明，他对专注的志愿者发出的提示也很敏感。汉斯在主人于1909年6月去世后，转到一位珠宝商手中，此人仍坚信马具有推理能力。
