Microplastics: Everywhere, but How Harmful?

微塑料：无处不在，危害几何？

In May 2004 Richard Thompson, a marine biologist at the University of Plymouth, and colleagues described an overlooked pollutant in the journal Science. Sifting through plankton samples kept since the 1960s, they found that microscopic plastic fragments and synthetic fibres had grown more abundant. They labelled the particles microplastics.

2004年5月，普利茅斯大学的海洋生物学家理查德·汤普森和同事在《科学》杂志上撰文，介绍了一种此前少有人注意的污染物。他们细细筛查自20世纪60年代起保存下来的浮游生物样本，发现极其微小的塑料碎片和合成纤维变得越来越多。他们把这些颗粒称为“微塑料”。

Since then the durable debris has proved ubiquitous, turning up on shorelines, in deep-sea sediment, in rainfall and in Arctic ice. Regulation came before proof of harm to people: in December 2015 America passed a law banning plastic microbeads from scrubs and other cosmetics rinsed down drains.

此后，这些经久不烂的碎屑被证明无所不在，海岸线上、深海沉积物里、雨水中和北极的冰层中都有它们的踪迹。人体受害尚无证据，监管就已先行：2015年12月，美国立法禁止在磨砂膏等用后被冲进下水道的化妆品中使用塑料微珠。

The search turned inward. In March 2022 a small Dutch study found plastic in the blood of about three-quarters of 22 healthy donors. In March 2024 a study in the New England Journal of Medicine linked plastic in the fatty plaque of neck arteries to a higher risk of heart attack, stroke or death. Its authors cautioned that correlation is not causation.

人们的搜寻随后转向了体内。2022年3月，荷兰一项小规模研究检测了22名健康献血者的血液，约四分之三的人血中有塑料。2024年3月，《新英格兰医学杂志》上的一项研究发现，颈部动脉的脂肪性斑块中含有塑料，与心脏病发作、中风或死亡的风险升高有关。研究者提醒，相关性并不等于因果关系。

The most sensational figures are the shakiest. In June 2019 a report for WWF, a conservation group, reckoned people might swallow a credit card's worth of plastic a week; critics call that an overestimate. Two months later the World Health Organization was reassuring but tentative: on limited evidence, microplastics in drinking water posed no apparent risk at current levels. In February 2025 a study in Nature Medicine reported plastic in brain tissue in an amount popularised as a spoonful; others queried its methodology.

最耸人听闻的数字，也最站不住脚。2019年6月，一份为自然保护组织世界自然基金会（WWF）撰写的报告估算，人们每周可能吞下相当于一张信用卡的塑料；批评者则说这是高估。两个月后，世界卫生组织的结论令人安心，却只是初步的：依据有限的证据，按目前的含量，饮用水中的微塑料并无明显风险。2025年2月，医学期刊《自然·医学》上的一项研究报告在脑组织中发现了塑料，其含量后来被传成脑中有一勺塑料；另有学者对它的研究方法提出了质疑。

Detection has outrun understanding; human studies remain small or observational. Harm is plausible but unproven, and until the verdict is in, better measurement may guide policy more safely than panic.

检测走在了认识前面；针对人体的研究规模仍小，或只是观察性的。说它有害合乎情理，却尚未证实；在定论出来之前，依据更好的测量来制定政策，或许比被恐慌左右更稳妥。
