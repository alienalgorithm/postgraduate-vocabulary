Survival in the Ice: The Legend of Endurance

冰海求生：坚忍号传奇

On 5 December 1914 Endurance departed South Georgia, a remote whaling outpost, for Antarctica's Weddell Sea. In January 1915 ice trapped the vessel. On 27 October, her hull buckling, the men abandoned ship. She foundered on 21 November; her captain, Frank Worsley, logged the spot.

1914年12月5日，坚忍号驶离南乔治亚岛——一处偏远的捕鲸前哨，开往南极洲的威德尔海。1915年1月，这艘船被冰困住。10月27日，船体开始扭曲变形，众人弃船。11月21日，船沉没；船长弗兰克·沃斯利把沉没地点记入了航海日志。

The stranded men endured over five gruelling months on the ice. Their floe split on 9 April 1916; on 15 April their lifeboats reached Elephant Island, a bleak, uninhabited rock far from shipping lanes.

进退无路的众人在冰上熬过了五个多月艰苦异常的日子。1916年4月9日，他们脚下的浮冰裂开；4月15日，他们的救生艇抵达象岛，那是一座荒凉、无人居住的岩岛，远离航线。

On 24 April Shackleton and five others embarked in the James Caird, a fragile lifeboat, on a perilous voyage of some 1,300 kilometres. As ferocious gales battered the boat, Worsley snatched only four sights of the sun, deducing the rest by dead reckoning from course, speed and time elapsed. On 10 May they landed on South Georgia's deserted south coast.

4月24日，沙克尔顿和另外五人登上单薄脆弱的救生艇詹姆斯·凯尔德号，踏上约1,300公里的凶险航程。狂暴的大风连番冲击着小艇，沃斯利只抓住间隙对太阳做了四次观测（即“测日”，航海上称 take a sight，据此定船位），其余都靠航位推算，根据航向、航速和经过的时间推断出来。5月10日，他们在南乔治亚岛空无一人的南岸登陆。

Shackleton, Worsley and Tom Crean traversed its mountainous interior in about 36 exhausting hours, reaching the Stromness whaling station on 20 May. Ice repelled three rescue attempts before the Yelcho, a Chilean tug, evacuated the 22 marooned men on 30 August. All 28 of Endurance's party survived their ordeal.

沙克尔顿、沃斯利和汤姆·克林用了约36个令人精疲力竭的小时，横越岛上山峦重叠的腹地，于5月20日抵达斯特罗姆内斯捕鲸站。浮冰三次逼退救援船，直到8月30日，智利的拖船耶乔号才把流落荒岛的22人撤出象岛。坚忍号一行28人全部挺过了这场磨难。

On 5 March 2022 searchers located the wreck 3,008 metres down, upright and intact. She lay about four miles south of the spot Worsley recorded in November 1915, months before the voyage: a slight discrepancy for a calculation on shifting ice.

2022年3月5日，搜寻队在3,008米深的海底找到了残骸，船身直立，完好无损。它位于沃斯利1915年11月所记位置以南约四英里处；这个位置是小艇出航前几个月测算的。冰面一直在漂移，在上面计算出的位置只有这么一点偏差，已属难得。

Legend rightly salutes Shackleton for his charisma. Yet both ends of the story hinge on Worsley's meticulous arithmetic. Leadership held 28 men together. Precision put six ashore within reach of help and, a century on, brought their ship back into view.

传说称颂沙克尔顿的超凡魅力，这理所当然。可这个故事的两头，都取决于沃斯利一丝不苟的算术。领导力让28人始终抱成一团；精确则把其中六人送上岸，送到了能求得救援的地方，一个世纪后，又让他们的船重回世人视野。
