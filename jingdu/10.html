考研英语精读 · No.10

The Stolen Smile

被偷走的微笑

On Monday 21 August 1911, when the Louvre was customarily closed, the Mona Lisa was discreetly plucked from its wall. The disappearance went unnoticed until the next day, when a painter arriving to sketch found a conspicuous gap of bare plaster. Custodians shuttered the museum for a week.

1911年8月21日是星期一，卢浮宫照例闭馆，《蒙娜丽莎》就在这一天被人从墙上悄悄摘走。画作的失踪一直无人察觉，直到第二天，一位前来画素描的画家发现，灰泥墙面上露出一块显眼的空缺。管理人员把博物馆关闭了一周。

When the museum reopened, curious onlookers queued to stare at the vacant space where the masterpiece had hung. On 7 September 1911 the poet Guillaume Apollinaire was held in custody for about a week, and Pablo Picasso was interrogated. Both were cleared of involvement in the burglary.

博物馆重新开放后，好奇的围观者排队前来，凝视那块空荡荡的地方，那幅杰作原先就挂在那里。1911年9月7日，诗人纪尧姆·阿波利奈尔被拘押了约一周，巴勃罗·毕加索也受到审问。两人都洗清了牵涉这起盗窃案的嫌疑。

The intruder was Vincenzo Peruggia, an Italian who had temporarily worked at the Louvre as a glazier. Having lurked in a storeroom on Sunday, he strode out unchallenged in a workman's smock, the painting tucked beneath his clothes. For more than two years it lay stashed in a false-bottomed trunk in his Paris lodgings.

闯入者是文森佐·佩鲁贾，一个曾在卢浮宫短期做过玻璃工的意大利人。他星期天潜伏在一间储藏室里，之后穿着一件工作罩衫大步走了出去，无人盘问，画就塞在他的衣服底下。此后两年多，这幅画一直藏匿在他巴黎住处一只带夹层底的箱子里。

In December 1913 Peruggia contacted Alfredo Geri, an art dealer in Florence. The Uffizi's director authenticated the painting at Peruggia's hotel; Peruggia was apprehended on 11 December 1913. Citing patriotism, he said he wanted it repatriated, mistakenly believing Napoleon had looted it. In fact Leonardo da Vinci had accompanied it to France at the invitation of King Francis I.

1913年12月，佩鲁贾联系了佛罗伦萨的艺术品商人阿尔弗雷多·杰里。乌菲齐美术馆馆长在佩鲁贾下榻的旅馆鉴定了这幅画，1913年12月11日，佩鲁贾被逮捕。他以爱国为由，说想把画送回祖国；他误以为拿破仑掠走了这幅画。事实上，列奥纳多·达·芬奇应国王弗朗索瓦一世的邀请，随身带着这幅画去了法国。

After showings in Italy, the painting was reinstated in the Louvre on 4 January 1914. In June 1914 a tribunal imposed a penalty of a year and 15 days, but he was imprisoned for about seven months. Many historians argue the heist elevated one esteemed gem among many to the world's most renowned painting. If so, its fame began with a bare wall.

这幅画在意大利展出之后，于1914年1月4日重回卢浮宫。1914年6月，法庭判处佩鲁贾一年零十五天的刑罚，但他实际坐牢约七个月。许多史学家认为，这幅画原本是众多备受推崇的珍品之一，这起盗画案把它推上了世界最著名画作的位置。若真如此，它的名气始于一面光秃秃的墙。
