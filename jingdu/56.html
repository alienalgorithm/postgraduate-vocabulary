考研英语精读 · No.56

The Letter That Startled Darwin

一封信惊醒了达尔文

Charles Darwin disembarked from HMS Beagle in October 1836. In September 1838 he began formulating the principle of natural selection: individuals better adapted to their surroundings survive and pass on their hereditary traits. By 1844 he had composed a 230-page manuscript, yet kept it unpublished, cautiously amassing evidence.

1836年10月，查尔斯·达尔文从小猎犬号上登岸。1838年9月，他开始提出自然选择的原理：对环境适应得更好的个体得以存活，并把自身的遗传性状传递下去。到1844年，他已写成一份230页的手稿，却一直未予发表，谨慎地积累证据。

In February 1858, two decades after Darwin's first insight, Alfred Russel Wallace, a naturalist afflicted by fever in the Malay Archipelago, independently conceived the same idea. His concise essay reached Darwin on 18 June 1858. Its argument paralleled Darwin's own almost precisely; he told Charles Lyell he had never seen a more remarkable coincidence. His claim to priority seemed threatened.

1858年2月，距达尔文最初的设想已有二十年，博物学家阿尔弗雷德·拉塞尔·华莱士在马来群岛身患热病，独立地构想出了同一个想法。华莱士那篇简明的文章于1858年6月18日寄到达尔文手中。文中的论证与达尔文本人的论证几乎完全相合；达尔文告诉查尔斯·莱尔，自己从未见过比这更惊人的巧合。他对优先权的主张似乎受到了威胁。

Lyell and the botanist Joseph Hooker arranged for both papers to be read simultaneously at the Linnean Society, a scientific institution in London, on 1 July 1858. Neither author attended the proceedings. Wallace was abroad, and Darwin was grieving: scarlet fever had killed his infant son on 28 June. The compromise, struck without Wallace, may have kept rivalry over precedence at bay.

莱尔和植物学家约瑟夫·胡克促成两篇论文于1858年7月1日在伦敦的科学机构林奈学会同时宣读。两位作者都没有出席当天的会议。华莱士身在海外，达尔文则处于哀痛之中：6月28日，猩红热夺去了他幼子的生命。这一折中办法在华莱士未参与的情况下达成，或许避免了围绕先后次序的竞争。

The papers' publication on 20 August 1858 met a muted reception. In May 1859 the Society's president remarked in his yearly address that the year had been barren of revolutionary discoveries. The sensation came on 24 November 1859 with Darwin's treatise, On the Origin of Species: booksellers had subscribed for every copy of its first impression, 1,250 in all.

1858年8月20日，两篇论文刊出，得到的反响却很平淡。1859年5月，学会会长在年度致辞中谈到，这一年毫无革命性的发现。1859年11月24日，达尔文的专著《物种起源》问世，引起轰动：书商们已认购了该书第一次印刷的每一册，共1,250册。

Wallace had readily signified his acceptance in October 1858, assuring Hooker in a cordial letter of his satisfaction with the arrangement. In 1889 he published a lengthy exposition of the idea and entitled it Darwinism. Wallace never named the theory after himself.

1858年10月，华莱士就已欣然表明了接受的态度，在一封亲切的信中向胡克保证，自己对这一安排感到满意。1889年，他出版了一部长篇论述阐释这一思想，并将其定名为《达尔文主义》。华莱士从未用自己的名字为这一理论命名。
