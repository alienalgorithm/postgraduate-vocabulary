考研英语精读 · No.45

The Record Humanity Threw into the Stars

写给外星人的一张唱片

In late summer 1977, NASA fastened a disc wrought from gilded copper, 30 centimetres wide, to each Voyager spaceship before takeoff. Nobody could foresee who, if anyone, would play one.

1977年夏末，NASA在每艘旅行者号宇宙飞船的起飞之前，给它固定上一张圆盘。圆盘用镀金的铜打造而成，宽30厘米。谁也无法预见，究竟会不会有人、又会是谁来播放其中一张。

Advisers under Carl Sagan of Cornell distilled our earthly abode into this compendium. Voices greet the listener in 55 languages, followed by the rumble of thunder, the whistle of wind, whales, footsteps, laughter and a rhythmic heartbeat. About 90 minutes of eclectic music cover a spectrum of cultures and eras, notably Bach and Chuck Berry's Johnny B. Goode.

康奈尔大学卡尔·萨根手下的顾问们，把我们在尘世的家园，提炼成这部汇编。人声用55种语言向听者致以问候，接着是隆隆的雷声、风的呼啸声、鲸歌、脚步声、笑声和富有节奏的心跳。约90分钟兼收并蓄的音乐，覆盖了多种文化与年代的广阔谱系，尤其值得一提的是巴赫和查克·贝里的《Johnny B. Goode》。

An array of 115 images, encoded in analogue form, sits beside printed words from two statesmen, Jimmy Carter and Kurt Waldheim. The sleeve gives guidance on playing the disc, plus a diagram fixing the Sun with pulsars as beacons.

一系列共115幅图像，以模拟方式编码，旁边印着两位政治家吉米·卡特与库尔特·瓦尔德海姆的文字。封套上给出播放的指引，还有一幅示意图，以脉冲星作信标标定太阳的位置。

On 14 February 1990, at Sagan's request, Voyager 1 pivoted its camera for a glance backwards across about six billion kilometres. Sixty frames formed a panorama of the solar system, a mosaic in which Earth was a glimmer about an eighth of a pixel across. They reached Earth's receivers between March and 1 May, each signal taking five and a half hours.

1990年2月14日，应萨根之请，旅行者1号转动相机，向后投去一瞥，跨越约60亿公里。60帧画面拼成太阳系的全景，在这幅拼图中，地球只是一点微光，宽约八分之一个像素。这些画面在3月至5月1日间抵达地球的接收设备，信号单程要走五个半小时。

In a 1994 book, Sagan offered that pale dot as a perspective on our small and ephemeral place amid cosmic vastness.

在1994年的一本书中，萨根把那个暗淡的小点作为一种视角，借以审视我们在宇宙的浩瀚之中渺小而短暂的位置。

Voyager 1 crossed into interstellar space in August 2012, the first human-made object there, and Voyager 2 followed in 2018. Both work on with a longevity unmatched at NASA, but depletion of their power should silence them within a few years.

2012年8月，旅行者1号进入星际空间，成为第一个到达那里的人造物体；旅行者2号于2018年跟进。两者仍在工作，其长寿在NASA无可匹敌，但电力耗竭预计会让它们在几年之内沉寂。

The Voyagers' discs will glide on outwards through the void as messengers with a map leading back to the Sun.

两艘旅行者号的唱片将作为信使，向外穿过虚空继续滑行，携着一幅指回太阳的地图。
