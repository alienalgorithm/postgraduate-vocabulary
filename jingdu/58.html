考研英语精读 · No.58

From a Scratch to a World Without Smallpox

从一道划痕到天花绝迹

Smallpox, a contagious affliction, killed about three in ten of its sufferers and an estimated 300 million in the 20th century. An early safeguard, deliberately implanting matter from its sores in the healthy, was practised in China by the 16th century and reached England in 1721. The procedure itself could cause grave sickness or death, if less frequently than natural smallpox.

天花是一种传染性病痛，约三成患者因此丧命；仅在20世纪，估计就有3亿人死于天花。一种早期的防护手段，是把天花脓疮中的物质有意植入健康人体内。到16世纪，中国已在施行这种方法，1721年这种方法又传入英国。这种做法本身也可能引起严重疾病乃至死亡，虽然不像自然感染的天花那样频繁。

Rustic lore held that milkmaids with cowpox, a mild ailment of livestock, were immune to smallpox. In 1774 Benjamin Jesty, a Dorset farmer, employed cowpox to protect his wife and two sons. On 14 May 1796 Edward Jenner, a provincial practitioner, inserted fluid from a milkmaid's cowpox sore into a scratch on eight-year-old James Phipps.

乡间传说认为，挤奶女工若得过牛痘这种牲畜的轻微病症，就对天花免疫。1774年，多塞特郡农夫本杰明·杰斯蒂使用牛痘保护妻子和两个儿子。1796年5月14日，地方医生爱德华·詹纳从一名挤奶女工的牛痘疮中取出液体，注入8岁的詹姆斯·菲普斯身上的一道划痕。

On 1 July 1796 Jenner exposed the boy to smallpox; no symptoms ensued. In 1797 the Royal Society declined to publish his paper, judging his evidence unconvincing. In June 1798 he privately funded his book, the Inquiry, with supplementary cases. The word vaccine derives from the Latin vacca, cow.

1796年7月1日，詹纳让这个男孩接触天花，任何症状都没有随之出现。1797年，皇家学会不予发表他的论文，认为他的证据缺乏说服力。1798年6月，詹纳私人出资出版了他的著作《探究》，书中附上了补充病例。表示疫苗的英文词源自拉丁语的 vacca，意思是“牛”。

Jenner was not the idea's originator. His contribution was a test, with results accessible in print, so other doctors could replicate and disseminate the technique. By today's standards, experimentation on a child without informed consent would be unethical. Jenner died on 26 January 1823, with smallpox far from eliminated.

詹纳并不是这一想法的首创者。他的贡献在于做了一项试验，并把结果付印，供人查阅，其他医生因而得以重复并推广这项技术。按今天的标准，拿孩子做实验而不取得知情同意，属于不道德行为。詹纳于1823年1月26日去世，那时天花还远未被消除。

In 1967 the WHO launched an intensified, concerted campaign to eradicate smallpox. The last naturally occurring case was diagnosed in Somalia on 26 October 1977. On 8 May 1980 the World Health Assembly formally declared the world free of smallpox, the only human disease ever wiped out. That pronouncement came almost 184 years after Phipps was vaccinated.

1967年，世界卫生组织发起了一场强化、协同的运动，要根除天花。最后一例自然发生的病例于1977年10月26日在索马里确诊。1980年5月8日，世界卫生大会正式宣布全世界已摆脱天花，天花也成为迄今唯一被消灭的人类疾病。这一宣告距菲普斯接种已近184年。
