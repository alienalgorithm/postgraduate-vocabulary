考研英语精读 · No.18

The Mould That Changed the World

改变人类命运的霉菌

In September 1928 Alexander Fleming, a bacteriologist at St Mary's Hospital, London, returned from holiday to find that a stray mould had contaminated a dish of staphylococcus bacteria. Nothing grew around it. Popular mythology blames an open window; the spores probably originated in a laboratory downstairs, where a colleague cultivated moulds.

1928年9月，伦敦圣玛丽医院的细菌学家亚历山大·弗莱明度假归来，发现一团不知从哪儿飘来的霉菌污染了一只培养葡萄球菌这种细菌的培养皿。霉菌周围什么也没长。坊间传说把它归咎于一扇敞开的窗户；其实孢子很可能来自楼下的实验室，一位同事在那里培育霉菌。

Fleming called the mould's juice penicillin and published in June 1929. Yet the crude juice was unstable and stubbornly hard to purify, and he and the chemists he enlisted lacked the expertise to refine it. He used it chiefly to isolate other microbes, and penicillin lay dormant, a curiosity rather than a cure.

弗莱明把霉菌分泌的这种液体称为青霉素，并于1929年6月发表了论文。然而，这种粗制的液体性质不稳定，又怎么也难以提纯；他和他请来的化学家都缺乏精炼它所需的专业知识。他主要用它来分离其他微生物，青霉素就此沉寂，只是件稀罕物，而非一种疗法。

A decade later, in Oxford, Howard Florey, an Australian pathologist, revived the work with Ernst Chain, a German-born chemist and Jewish refugee. Their colleague Norman Heatley improvised an ingenious apparatus to extract the compound. On 25 May 1940 eight mice received a lethal dose of bacteria; only the four also injected with penicillin lived. The Lancet, a prestigious journal, printed the outcome that August.

约十年后，在牛津，澳大利亚病理学家霍华德·弗洛里与德国出生的化学家、犹太难民恩斯特·钱恩一起，重启了这项研究。他们的同事诺曼·希特利用手边的材料临时拼凑出一套巧妙的装置，用来提取这种化合物。1940年5月25日，八只小鼠接种了致死剂量的细菌；只有另外注射了青霉素的那四只活了下来。同年8月，享有盛誉的期刊《柳叶刀》刊登了这一结果。

In February 1941 Albert Alexander, an Oxford constable gravely ill with infection, rallied on penicillin. When the scarce supply ran out, he died in March: the obstacle was quantity. From July 1941 a government laboratory in Peoria, Illinois, boosted yields about tenfold with corn-steep liquor, a starch by-product; later a mouldy market melon furnished the laboratory with a more prolific strain. By D-Day in June 1944 industrial fermentation met Allied needs.

1941年2月，牛津警员艾伯特·亚历山大因感染而病情危重，用上青霉素后有所好转。本就稀缺的青霉素用光后，他于3月不治身亡：障碍在于数量。从1941年7月起，伊利诺伊州皮奥里亚的一家政府实验室用玉米浆（淀粉生产的副产品）把产量提高了约十倍；后来，市场上一只发霉的甜瓜又为这家实验室提供了一株更高产的菌株。到1944年6月诺曼底登陆时，工业发酵已能满足盟军的需求。

In October 1945 Fleming, Florey and Chain shared the Nobel prize; Heatley was overlooked. Fortune put the mould in Fleming's dish; tenacious teams and years of toil transformed it into a remedy.

1945年10月，弗莱明、弗洛里和钱恩共同获得诺贝尔奖；希特利却遭到忽视。机缘把霉菌送进了弗莱明的培养皿；几支锲而不舍的团队，加上多年的辛劳，把它转化成了良药。
