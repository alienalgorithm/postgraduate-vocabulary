# Article 27 visual assets

These two SVGs are original explanatory diagrams created specifically for Article 27, *The Cable Stretched Every Pulse*.

## `pulse-dispersion.svg`

Conceptual mechanism diagram: short telegraph pulses enter a long submarine cable and emerge broadened, reducing the time separation between neighbouring symbols. The diagram is explanatory and not to scale.

Historical/technical references used for the mechanism:
- Cambridge University Press, *Wildman Whitehouse, William Thomson, and the First Atlantic Cable*
- William Thomson, 1858, *Atlantic Telegraph Instruments and Working*

## `mirror-galvanometer.svg`

Conceptual mechanism diagram of Thomson's mirror galvanometer: a tiny current deflects a suspended mirror; reflected light converts that small angular movement into a much larger displacement on a distant scale.

Historical/object reference:
- Science Museum Group, *Mirror galvanometer for the transatlantic telegraph, 1858*

## Rights

Both SVG files in this folder are original project artwork. They are not traced copies of museum photographs or historical engravings. Historical sources were used only to verify the mechanism and object arrangement described by the article.
